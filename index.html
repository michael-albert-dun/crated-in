Crated In

The door slams behind you, and now you're crated in. Across the room, a column of light glows: your only way out.

It should be easy: you have a wand that's supposed to move crates around for you. But it doesn't seem to be working properly! All it does is destroy the top of a nearby stack that you can't climb and put copies down on its neighbours.

How to play

  • Climb. You can clamber up or down one crate at a time. Dropping down two or more is too far.
  • Point. Point the wand at a stack that's too tall to climb (by pushing against it). The crate on top is destroyed, but a copy of it drifts down onto each neighbouring stack, including yours, so you're lifted a level.
  • Move. Use arrow keys or WASD, or tap a neighbouring square. z undoes, r restarts, c peeks at a solution and m comes back here.
