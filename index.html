Crated In

The door slams behind you, and now you're crated in. Across the room, a column of light glows: your only way out.

It should be easy: you have a wand that's supposed to move crates that are too high to reach. But it doesn't seem to be working properly! Instead of just moving a crate from the top of a nearby stack, it makes copies and puts them down nearby.

  • Climb. You can clamber up or down one crate at a time. Pushing against a stack that's too tall to climb uses the wand.
  • Move. Use arrow keys or WASD, or tap a neighbouring square. z undoes, r restarts, c peeks at a solution and m comes back to the levels.
