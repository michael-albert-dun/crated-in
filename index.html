Crated In

Levels and Settings

You're trapped in a room and want the way out (the lit doorway; stepping through it leads on to the next room). You can clamber up or down one crate at a time. Dropping down two or more is too far. Push against a stack that's too tall to climb and its top crate copies itself, the copies drift down onto the neighbouring stacks, and you're lifted a level. Move with arrow keys or WASD, or tap a neighbouring square. c peeks at a solution.
